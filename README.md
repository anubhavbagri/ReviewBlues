## ReviewBlues
An extension which unlocks rewards for giving honest reviews, building a trusted feedback loop.

## Why do you want to work on this ❓

remember that amazing product you almost bought, only to discover later it looked nothing like the photos thanks to sketchy reviews? we've all been there. as an online buyer myself, i was tired of wading through endless reviews, unsure if they're genuine or just rants. that's why i came up with reviewblues, a browser extension that rewards genuine feedback with exclusive deals. imagine getting rewarded for helping others and saving on your next purchase! let's make online shopping smarter and more rewarding, together.
